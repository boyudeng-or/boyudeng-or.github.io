Boyu Deng

I am a first-year PhD student in Data Science and Engineering at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville (Bredesen Center / UTK–ORNL), advised by Prof. Hongyu Zheng. My research focuses on mathematical modeling and control for mixed-traffic systems—where human-driven and automated vehicles share the same road.

Before UTK, I earned an MSc in Mathematics at The Chinese University of Hong Kong and completed my undergraduate studies at Jinan University in Guangzhou. I grew up in Huludao, China.

I am especially interested in car-following dynamics, safety-oriented control, and models that stay useful when traffic is only partly automated.
