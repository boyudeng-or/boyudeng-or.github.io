UTK Bredesen Center · Data Science and Engineering

Boyu Deng

I am a first-year PhD student in Data Science and Engineering at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville (Bredesen Center / UTK–ORNL), advised by Prof. Hongyu Zheng.

I work at the intersection of mathematics, data, and decision-making. My interests are still taking shape; the themes below sketch the space I care about most.

Before Knoxville, I studied mathematics at The Chinese University of Hong Kong and completed my undergraduate degree at Jinan University in Guangzhou. I grew up in Huludao, China.
